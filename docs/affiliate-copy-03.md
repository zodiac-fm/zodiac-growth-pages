# Affiliate copy revision 03
November 16, 2026. Draft for review. No prior version was overwritten.

Natasha asked for a rewrite for the right audiences, with the tone “woo woo, luxury, smart, but simple” and “sort of mormonish.” When asked to distinguish Hormozi-style persuasion from a Mormon-inspired tone, she selected Mormon-inspired. This revision interprets that as warm spiritual conviction, belonging, purpose, and a personal nature worth returning to. It introduces no church affiliation, scripture, or new religious doctrine. This is a scoped user direction for these drafts, not a change to the brand's global voice or founder-approved standing copy.

## Central arguments
Astrology: a chart is one way of meeting yourself, and music can be another. The headline is “Come home to the self your chart describes.” The argument moves from the recognition of a reading to returning to that sense of yourself during ordinary life.

Meditation: personal music can accompany the space someone already makes for themselves. The headline is “Let the music bring you back to yourself.” The argument honors the existing practice and makes room for everyday listening. Meditation is welcome and optional.

The membership is a personal library to return to. Five longer compositions are included alongside the three short ones that remain free. Its value is framed around sustained listening, while the actual additional access remains explicit. Neither annual nor monthly is preselected.
