A few things before you begin.

Will I get an astrology reading?

You'll get your four personal frequencies and music tuned to them. Zodiac doesn't interpret chart placements, predict events, or replace a reading.

Does the music change with transits?

Your four frequencies stay fixed. Zodiac doesn't add current transits to the music. The idea is to keep a personal reference as life changes.

What if I don't know my birth time?

Use your best estimate if you have one. If you have no idea, Zodiac’s current guidance is to use 12:00 noon. Your time affects the calculation, so an accurate time is preferable.

Do I need to meditate or know Human Design?

No. You can listen alongside a practice or during an ordinary day. You don't need chart knowledge or Human Design terminology.
