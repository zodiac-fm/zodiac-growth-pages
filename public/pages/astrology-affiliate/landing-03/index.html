A few things you may want to know.

Will this give me another reading?

Zodiac gives you personal music, rather than an interpretation of your chart. Readings can remain part of how you understand yourself. The music offers an experience you can return to alongside them.

Do I need to know Human Design?

No. If Human Design is part of your life, Zodiac can sit alongside it. You don't need its language, or any chart knowledge, to listen.

What if I don't know my birth time?

Use your best estimate, or 12:00 noon if you have no idea. Time affects the calculation, so an accurate time is preferable.

What should I expect when I begin?

Start with music you enjoy and a time you can return to. About nine minutes a day is the starting guidance, and the app helps balance your four frequencies through the week. There isn't a required feeling or an immediate result you have to notice.
