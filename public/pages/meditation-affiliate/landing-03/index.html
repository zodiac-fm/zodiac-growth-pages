Do I need to meditate?

No. Zodiac is music. You can listen during meditation, while you write, or during an ordinary part of your day. There's no new practice you have to learn.

Why does it use birth information?

Birth date, time, and place are how Zodiac calculates the four frequencies carried in your music. This is a birth-based, metaphysical approach. You don't need astrology knowledge, but that approach is part of what you're choosing to explore.

What if I don't know my birth time?

Use your best estimate, or 12:00 noon if you have no idea. Time affects the calculation, so an accurate time is preferable.

Can I keep listening without a membership?

Yes. Quiet Success, Kindred Spirits, and Elysian Surge remain free in all four of your personal frequencies. Full membership includes the five longer compositions too.
