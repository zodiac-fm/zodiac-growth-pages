What to try.

What changed.

The astrology argument connects a reading's self-understanding with everyday choices. The meditation argument starts with the time someone has for a listen, then explains birth-based personalization. Longer paid compositions now have names, lengths, and approved descriptions.

Approved quotations appear without unverified portraits. Existing partner IDs, vanity URLs, coupons, and A/G/M categories remain unchanged. A meditation concept doesn't reclassify category M.
