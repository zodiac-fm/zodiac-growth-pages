Why does a music experience ask when you were born?

Birth details are how Zodiac personalizes the frequencies in the music. The approach draws on the Vedic idea of Nāda Brahma, that the world is sound, and a protected frequency method drawn from a mantra lineage.

In Zodiac's model, a pattern from your birth moment can be expressed as four frequencies. Listening to them in music is intended to strengthen your connection to yourself.

You don't need to read a birth chart. This is a metaphysical proposition to explore, not scientific proof of a medical or physiological effect.

Personalized means something specific here.

Your birth date, time, and place determine the four frequencies. Musicians make the songs, and Zodiac retunes each one so your frequency becomes part of its harmonies.

The frequencies stay fixed. They aren't chosen from your mood or a relaxation preference.

Is this a meditation course?

Zodiac is personalized music. It can accompany meditation, journaling, or another practice, and it doesn't require any of them.

Do I have to believe in astrology?

You don't need astrology knowledge. Birth-based personalization is part of the product, and its basis is metaphysical. If you're curious about that approach, listening is a way to explore it for yourself.

Do I need headphones?

You can use headphones or speakers. Choose what fits your space and the way you like to listen.

What if I don't know my birth time?

Use your best estimate if you have one. If you have no idea, Zodiac’s current guidance is to use 12:00 noon. Your time affects the calculation, so an accurate time is preferable.

Can I listen without a paid membership?

The three short songs in each of your four frequencies remain free. Membership gives you access to the full music library, including the longer songs.
