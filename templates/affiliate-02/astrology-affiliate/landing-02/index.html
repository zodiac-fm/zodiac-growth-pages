What you might still be wondering.

Is this an astrology or Human Design reading?

Zodiac is a music experience. It doesn't interpret chart placements or deliver forecasts. If you use Human Design, it can sit alongside that exploration too. You don't need its terminology to listen.

Do my frequencies change with transits?

Your four personal frequencies stay fixed. Zodiac doesn't add current transits to the music. The idea is to keep a personal reference as life and the sky change.

What if I don't know my birth time?

Use your best estimate if you have one. If you have no idea, Zodiac's current guidance is to use 12:00 noon. An accurate time is preferable because time affects the calculation.

How should I begin listening?

Choose music you enjoy and a time you can return to. About nine minutes a day is the starting guidance, and the app helps balance your four frequencies through the week. There isn't a required bodily sensation or a guaranteed first-listen result.

Can I stop before I'm charged?

Yes. Cancel before day {{billingDay}} to avoid the first membership charge. After that, cancellation stops the next renewal. Paid charges aren't refunded. The three short compositions in each frequency stay free.
