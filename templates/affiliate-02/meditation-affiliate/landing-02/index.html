Why your birth details belong in the music.

An ordinary playlist can follow a mood. Zodiac starts with your birth date, time, and place, using them to find four personal frequencies that stay fixed.

Musicians make the compositions. Each one is retuned so your frequency becomes part of its harmonies. The intention is to strengthen your connection to your own nature, including the sense of what fits when you're making a choice or meeting a change.

This approach draws on the Vedic idea of Nāda Brahma, that the world is sound, and a protected frequency method from a mantra lineage. It's a metaphysical model to explore, rather than established science. You don't need to read a birth chart to listen.

Give the music a place in your week.

You can keep your practice and add the music. Meditation isn't required, so it can follow you into an ordinary part of the day too.

  1. Choose a listen you want to come back to.

    Starseed and Astrolith are nine-minute compositions. Temple Space gives you 30 minutes for a longer practice. Pick the music and length that suit the time you have.

  2. Let the app help balance the frequencies.

    All four belong in your week. About nine minutes a day is the starting guidance, rather than a test you have to pass.

  3. Decide whether you'd like to keep listening.

    Notice what you enjoy and whether you want to return. There isn't a required sensation or a promised change by the end of the free week.

Make sure it fits your practice.

Is this a meditation course?

Zodiac is music. It can accompany the meditation, journaling, or other practice you already use. There are no new meditation instructions to learn.

Do I have to accept astrology to listen?

You don't need astrology knowledge. The product's basis is birth-based and metaphysical, so that is part of what you're choosing to explore. It isn't a scientific test of astrology.

Do I need headphones?

Suitable headphones or quality speakers can carry the recordings. Use comfortable volume and choose equipment that reproduces the music clearly.

What if I don't know my birth time?

Use your best estimate if you have one. If you have no idea, Zodiac's current guidance is to use 12:00 noon. An accurate time is preferable because time affects the calculation.

What can I keep without paying?

Quiet Success, Kindred Spirits, and Elysian Surge remain free in all four of your personal frequencies. The five longer compositions come with full membership.
